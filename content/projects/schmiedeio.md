As the lead developer and designer, I conceptualized and executed a comprehensive digital transformation for the [Schmiede website](https://schmiedehallein.com/), focusing on accessibility, usability, and community engagement. Utilizing a modern stack of React and Tailwind CSS, I built a web-based system where users can log in, apply for Schmiede, and interact through a dedicated social feed designed for active post-sharing and collaboration. I steered the project through every stage of development—from the first wireframes and local backend API design to rigorous Postman endpoint testing and the final deployment on render.com—resulting in an all-in-one platform that seamlessly manages event logistics, volunteer coordination, and participant communication.
