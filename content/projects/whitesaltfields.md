White Salt fields is a short documentary video essay about Vietnam, questioning the concepts of home, origin, and identity.
In an era of accelerating technology and hyper-complexity, what does it mean to be "at home"? What does the question "Where do you come from?" truly signify, and what does it mean to find oneself in a "hometown"? I address these core inquiries in this video essay about Vietnam—a country defined by its astonishing natural beauty, cultural complexities, dynamic megacities, and remote ethnic hill-tribe villages.

To expand the work into a multi-sensory experience, I accompany the video essay with a live DJ set composed of abstract club tracks designed to construct futuristic, sci-fi-like soundscapes. 

Through this hybrid film screening and concert, my musical project Shinsekai (新世界, "New World") came to life and investigates new possibilities within the art of DJing. By playing and mixing contrasting genres from the dark side of electronic dance music, my goal is to dismantle the rigid boundaries of genre, taste, and social division. Driven by alien timbres, distorted vocals, and broken, club-oriented rhythms, the project offers a kaleidoscopic view of our musical futures.
