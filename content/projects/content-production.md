I founded and co-hosted an interactive lab dedicated to constructive and positive digital content creation. The initiative equips participants with viral marketing skills to craft and share culturally relevant media. As part of the workshop, creators live-stream their content directly to active community platforms, including [Youtube](https://www.youtube.com/@SchmiedeHalleinOfficial), [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/schmiedefestival) and [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@schmiede.hallein).

I also ran around and documented the 10 days and put it together in a short video, that documents the vibe of the festival.
