Promoter and Cultural Producer for conceptual party series with a focus on experimental soundscapes and rave experiences.

Based on the famous party which never happened, [FYRE Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fyre_Festival),
GLOBAL FYRE 2099™ is a conceptual event series that deconstructs the "new normal" through the "dismemberment" and recreation of electronic club music. The project crafts immersive audio-visual rave experiences—spanning both offline and online environments—that explore unknown sonic possibilities and foster a community rooted in digital peace and unity. By prioritizing experimental soundscapes, the initiative seeks to transcend traditional club boundaries and redefine the contemporary rave experience.
