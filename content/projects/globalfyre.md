GLOBAL FYRE 2099™ is a conceptual event series that deconstructs the "new normal" through the deliberate dismemberment and recreation of electronic club music. The project crafts immersive audio-visual rave experiences—spanning both offline and online environments—that explore unknown sonic possibilities and foster a community rooted in digital peace and unity. By prioritizing experimental soundscapes, the initiative seeks to transcend traditional club boundaries and redefine the contemporary rave experience.
