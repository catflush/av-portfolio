_Underground Resistance_ investigates non-human kinship and biofeedback through live performance, DIY methodologies, and self-experimentation. I developed this project during a **V2\_ Summer Sessions** residency, the project emerged from the global shift toward DIY hobbyism during the COVID-19 pandemic.
Structure:

- Video proposal: A non-human video essay
- Documentation of process in the form of a zine with manual
- AUDIO GUIDE: "Listening Exercise"
