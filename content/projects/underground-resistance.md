Application video for V2 summer sessions residency 2021. The idea was to investigates non-human kinship and biofeedback through live performance, DIY methodologies, and self-experimentation.

I got accepted into the residency and produced an How to manueal
_Underground Resistance_  I developed this project during a **V2\_ Summer Sessions** residency, the project emerged from the global shift toward DIY hobbyism during the COVID-19 pandemic.

Text:

```
Our cultures do not encourage us
to think much about learning.
Our evolution works with mindless 
disrespect for individuals.
We humans have shared our universe 
with many intelligences, but 
downgraded any form of 
intelligence who does not 
resemble our narrow definition 
of intelligence.
As a result, we consume, despoil, 
and poison their natural habitat.
Driving in an ignorant vehicle on 
a road which leads to devastation 
of our own planet and to 
our own extinction.
Our environments are 
open ended assemblages 
of non-humans, living and non-living, 
entangled in ways of life.
What is the relationship between 
human and non-human entities, and 
how is it possible to build a 
bridge between micro and macro cosmos?
Think of yourself as a fungi.
Fungi are very old, older than 
our own species. 
History. They are very diverse, 
flexible and live in so many places, 
from the ocean to our own toenails. 
But most of them live in the soil
in an underground city. 
Fungi are more like animals and 
must find something to eat to survive. 
But instead of killing others, 
they are more generous and create worlds for others to survive and coexist.
To my residency, 
I want to contribute to the 
development of a culture 
which is based on a symbiosis, 
rather than following the 
survival of the fittest narrative.
The goal of my project 
uses artistic research 
methods to investigate fungi live on stage. 
I want to develop a tool 
which translates 
the bioactivity of fungus. 
I want to amplify their 
electrical activity 
to create sonic fiction of founders.
```
