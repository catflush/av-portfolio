Promoter and Cultural Producer for conceptual party series with a focus on experimental soundscapes and rave experiences.
This campaign paired visuals and messaging to support visibility for local FLINTA artists.

I successfully applied for the [Linz Sounds Award](https://www.linz.at/kultur/linz_sounds.php), a public funding program by the City of Linz, to launch the Linz_sound project with my interdisciplinary collective, P0$T0PIA_404. Guided by the belief that club culture is a catalyst for progressive social change and hybrid electronic art, I utilized the funding to challenge a male-dominated local scene by establishing a supportive network dedicated to empowering female, non-binary, queer, and marginalized newcomer DJs and bedroom producers.

Through this project, I orchestrated a dual offline and online strategy to connect local subcultures with the global digital music scene. Offline, I organized weekly technical training sessions, monthly pre-club practice nights, educational workshops with international collectives, and larger showcase events at local venues. Online, I managed digital residencies and a synchronized network across [SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/pyuk-edp) and [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/pyuk.edp/) to broadcast live streams, successfully positioning Linz's overlooked talent within the international electronic music community.
